PRACTICE NOTES

AI playbook

A lightweight, human-led path for bringing useful tools into creative work.

01⌕

Start with the friction

Observe the work. Ask designers what repeats, what gets lost, and what slows a decision down. Frame the problem before choosing a tool.

DISCOVERY3 prompts ↗
02✧

Test the smallest assist

Compare tools on a real, low-risk task. Check output quality, control, privacy, accessibility and the cleanup work a suggestion creates.

EXPERIMENTEvaluation canvas ↗
03⤴

Fit it into the flow

Keep inputs and outputs close to existing design files. Make it easy to review, edit and ignore AI-generated suggestions.

INTEGRATIONWorkflow map ↗
04▤

Share what you learn

Document where a tool helps, where it fails and what needs human judgment. Collect team feedback and revisit the experiment.

ADOPTIONTeam guide ↗
✳

Good AI integration feels like a better workflow. The team stays in control of the brief, the craft and the final call.
